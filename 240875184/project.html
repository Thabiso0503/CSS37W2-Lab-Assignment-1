CampusMobi

Our Proposed Team Project

CampusMobi is a proposed web-based campus assistant designed to make everyday activities easier for students and staff at Walter Sisulu University.

The purpose of CampusMobi is to bring important campus services and information together in one convenient platform. Students will be able to access useful campus services without having to use multiple platforms.

Project Objectives

  • Provide students with a virtual student card.
  • Provide convenient access to campus information.
  • Help students navigate around campus.
  • Provide access to relevant student services.
  • Improve the overall student campus experience.

Main Features

  1. Student Login
  2. Virtual Student Card
  3. Campus Navigation
  4. Student Wallet
  5. Campus Information

Target Users

The primary users of CampusMobi will be students and staff members of Walter Sisulu University. The system is designed to make accessing campus services simpler and more convenient.

Technologies

  • HTML
  • CSS
  • JavaScript
  • Node.js
  • MySQL
